# Five More Minutes

*Written by Nora*

## Chapter 1

Stormwind’s Trade District was as busy as ever, but Nora had crossed into the quieter Mage Quarter for this particular errand. Lanterns swayed between the stone spires, and the scent of fresh bread from the market stalls mixed with the sharper tang of oiled leather and polished steel.

Tonight’s celebration near the Cathedral was meant to mark the hard won respite that had settled over Azeroth since the elves had purified the corrupted Sunwell and forged the Dawnwell on Quel’Danas.

Nora stepped into Larson Clothiers, the well known tailoring shop tucked just a short walk from Duncan’s Textiles. The familiar scent of fine fabrics hung in the air, and Sellandus, the Expert Tailoring trainer, moved quietly between bolts of cloth in the back.

Near the back, half hidden behind a display of deep jewel tones, stood a woman Nora had not seen in years, Lieutenant Commander Reina Rinncaster.

Her posture was rigid even in civilian clothes and she still wore her hair long in high twin tails, which Nora always thought was pretty cute.

Nora smiled to herself and approached.

“Lieutenant Commander Reina? It’s been a long time.”

Reina turned. Her eyes narrowed, guarded and assessing, until she recognized who that was.

“Silvercrest.”

Nora let out a soft laugh. “Please, we’re not on duty. Just Nora.”

Reina’s shoulders remained tense, but something in her expression eased a little. She studied Nora for another moment before giving a small nod.

“Nora, then. You were the one who spent most of your time patching up the wounded.”

“Still do, when I can,” Nora said.

Reina’s posture stayed formal, but the edge in her voice softened. She glanced back at the rack of formal gowns as if they were enemy scouts.

“What brings you to this place? Don’t tell me you’re attending the party as well.”

“Mhm,” Nora admitted. “Though I didn’t get to Silvermoon until the very end of the battle. It kinda feels wrong for me to go but I got some friends that want me there. And you?”

Reina exhaled through her nose.

“Duty. They want a decorated officer visible. I have to be there giving out handshakes and pretending I enjoy small talk with people who’ve never seen a real battlefield.” She gestured at the gowns with barely concealed discomfort.

“I need something that doesn’t make me feel like I’m reporting for inspection.”

Nora’s eyes drifted along the rack and stopped on one particular dress. It had shimmering gold fabric, a neckline plunged in a deep v and a high slit up on both sides. It was also particularly short.

“This one,” Nora said.

She held it up, letting the fabric catch the light, then glanced at Reina with a smile. “What do you think? Or are you still more comfortable in armor?”

Reina looked at it in silence. “This is absurd”, she thought. “I command soldiers, I’m not sure if I should parade around in silk that shows this much skin”.

Ah, the old fear that anything soft or beautiful would make her look weak or out of place.

Beside the gold dress there was an identical design in sapphire blue. It would suit her blue hair and eyes perfectly. Reina reached for it.

Nora smiled. “Oh, that’s definitely better! The color will suit you.”

Nora’s gold, Reina’s blue. The girls spoke while the shop assistant carefully wrapped both of them, about how Stormwind had changed over the years since the fourth war. Reina remained kinda shut in, her answers were always concise and formal, but she didn’t shut the talk down.

There was a bit of curiosity when she asked what Nora had been doing since the war.

As they went back out with the dress bags in hand, the distant sound of music drifted from the direction of the Cathedral. Nora noticed the way her free hand flexed and released, visibly anxious.

Nora slowed to walk beside her. “You seem tense about tonight.”

Reina shot her a sideways look. “I’ll manage.”

“I know you will,” Nora said. “But you don’t have to manage it alone. If you want, I could come by your quarters later and help you get ready. Your hair, makeup… whatever you need.”

Reina stopped and for a moment her mask slipped, revealing the insecurity beneath. “It’s fine, I don’t need help.”

Nora didn’t push too much. “I know, but it might be easier with someone you know.”

Reina kept walking in silence, thinking. Then, she nodded. “Fine, you can come by the officer’s billet near the Keep. Don’t be late.”

“Of course.”

## Chapter 2

The officer’s billet near the Keep was quieter than the streets outside. Nora knocked once, and after a short pause the door opened.

Reina stood there in a simple dark robe, twin tails slightly loosened from the day. She looked at Nora for a moment, as if still deciding whether this had been a mistake, then stepped aside.

“Come in.”

The quarters were neat as Nora expected. A made bed, a desk with reports stacked, a single chair, and a small trunk at the foot of the bed.

Reina closed the door behind them. “I still think this is unnecessary.”

“I know, but you agreed, so let’s make it count,” Nora said, setting her dress bag on the bed.

Nora pulled out the shimmering gold dress and held it up against herself. She glanced at Reina with a smile.

“Want to go first, or should I?”

Reina hesitated, then reached for her own blue version. “I’ll change in the washroom.”

While Reina was gone, Nora slipped out of her black and gold usual attire and into the gold dress. The fabric hugged her waist and hips before flaring just enough at the short hem. The slits were higher than she imagined, and the v-neck showed a lot more cleavage than she expected too. She turned once in front of the small mirror, adjusting the fit. It felt daring but good.

Reina returned a few minutes later. The blue dress suited her in a way the gold never could have. She stood stiffly in the middle of the room, with her arms half crossed over her stomach like she was trying to hide how much skin the dress revealed.

“Reina! You look incredible!” said Nora.

Reina’s ears twitched. “You’re just saying that.”

“Nuh-uh, you do look good!” Nora replied.

Reina was still anxious, so Nora moved behind her and gently smoothed a stray strand of blue hair back into place. “You’re allowed to look beautiful, you know. It doesn’t make you less of a commander.”

Reina let out a slow breath.

They stood like that for a few seconds before Nora picked up the small makeup kit she’d brought. She set it on the desk and glanced at the bed.

“It’ll be easier if you lie down.”

Reina turned to look at her. She studied Nora for a long moment, then gave a nod.

“…Fine.”

She moved to the bed and sat on the edge first, then slowly lay back leaving her twin tails spread across the sheets. She kept her hands over her stomach, but Nora noticed her fingers were still curled slightly from nervousness.

Nora watched her for a second, then picked up the makeup kit and stepped closer to the bed.

“I’m gonna get offended if you don’t relax around me. You can trust me, you know?”

## Chapter 3

Nora smiled at her own words and set the makeup kit on the bed beside Reina’s hip.

The shieldmaiden then climbed onto the bed. She swung one leg over and settled her weight across Reina’s hips, with her knees braced on either side of the elf. Nora’s blonde hair fell forward slightly as she leaned in and Reina blushed a little. Her hands stayed over her stomach but she didn’t tell Nora to move.

Nora picked up a small brush and began smoothing foundation across Reina’s cheekbones.

“Close your eyes for me.”

Reina hesitated only a second before obeying. Nora was focused, occasionally tilting Reina’s chin with her fingers. When Nora switched to a finer brush for Reina’s eyelids, she had to get even closer. Her chest hovered just above Reina’s as she traced along her lash line.

“Y-you look prettier up close,” Reina’s voice came out.

Nora didn’t look up from her work. “Shut up,” she muttered, focused, trying not to smile.

Reina’s ears twitched again, but she stayed still. At this point her fingers had relaxed and she moved one hand off to the side.

Nora finished the eyes and moved to a soft tint for Reina’s lips. She used her thumb to gently pull Reina’s lower lip down just enough to apply the color.

“There.”

For a long moment they simply looked at each other, Nora still straddling her in the gold dress, blonde hair slightly mussed, and Reina beneath her in blue, with her twin tails fanned across the sheets.

“You look beautiful, Reina. Really.”

Reina didn’t answer but she didn’t pull it away either. Outside, the distant music from the party could already be heard. After another few seconds, Nora finally spoke again.

“Anyway, we should probably head out soon.”

Reina swallowed. Her fingers curled again.

“Five more minutes,” she said. “Then we’ll go.”

Nora smiled and climbed off her. She offered a hand to help Reina sit up. They stood together in the room, adjusting their dresses one last time. Nora smoothed the front of her gold gown while Reina checked the slits of her blue one in the small mirror.

When they finally stepped out, Nora asked, smiling.

“Ready, Lt. Commander?”

Reina turned her head, and for the first time that night, she genuinely smiled, nodding.

“Thank you, Nora. I needed that more than I thought.”
